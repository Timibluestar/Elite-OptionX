Trading Glossary

Trading Terms A–Z

Search common financial and trading terms to better understand market concepts.

Ask Price

The price at which a seller is willing to sell an asset.

Bid Price

The highest price a buyer is willing to pay for an asset.

Candlestick

A chart type showing open, high, low and close prices over a period.

Drawdown

The decline in account value from a peak before recovery.

Forex

The global market where currencies are exchanged.

Leverage

Borrowed capital that increases market exposure and also increases risk.

Margin

The amount required to open and maintain a leveraged position.

Pip

A standard unit used to measure price movement in many currency pairs.

Resistance

A price area where upward movement may slow or reverse.

Support

A price area where downward movement may slow or reverse.

Trend

The general direction in which the market is moving.

Trading Strategies

Explore Common Trading Strategies

Learn about widely used trading approaches, their characteristics, and when traders may choose to use them.

Trend Following

Focuses on identifying and trading in the direction of an established market trend.

Difficulty: Beginner Timeframe: Medium–Long

Breakout Trading

Looks for price movements beyond support or resistance levels that may signal increased market activity.

Difficulty: Intermediate Timeframe: Short–Medium

Range Trading

Involves identifying price ranges where markets repeatedly move between support and resistance.

Difficulty: Beginner Timeframe: Short

Swing Trading

Aims to capture price movements that develop over several days or weeks.

Difficulty: Intermediate Timeframe: Medium

Price Action

Uses price movement and chart patterns instead of relying solely on technical indicators.

Difficulty: Advanced Timeframe: Flexible

Risk Management

Covers position sizing, stop-loss placement, and portfolio management techniques.

Difficulty: Essential Applies to All
Market Analysis

Understanding Market Analysis

Market analysis helps traders evaluate financial markets using different methods. Learning these concepts can improve your understanding of market behavior.

Technical Analysis

Technical analysis studies historical price movements, chart patterns, volume and indicators to better understand market behavior.

  • ✔ Candlestick Charts
  • ✔ Trend Lines
  • ✔ Support & Resistance
  • ✔ Moving Averages

Fundamental Analysis

Fundamental analysis focuses on economic news, company performance and global events that may influence financial markets.

  • ✔ GDP Reports
  • ✔ Inflation
  • ✔ Interest Rates
  • ✔ Company Earnings

Risk Management

Risk management involves planning position sizes, understanding leverage and protecting capital with appropriate controls.

  • ✔ Position Sizing
  • ✔ Stop Loss
  • ✔ Diversification
  • ✔ Risk/Reward
